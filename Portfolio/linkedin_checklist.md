> **Course Outcome Alignment:** Q4 – CO4 (Professional personal branding, industry networking, portfolio presentation, and career readiness).

## 1. High-Impact Headline Formulas

Your headline is the first thing recruiters and engineering managers see. Avoid generic titles like *"Student at University"*; use formulaic, keyword-dense value propositions.

### Recommended Headline Options:
- **Option 1 (DevOps & Systems Focus - Recommended):**  
  `DevOps & Systems Engineer | Linux Administration • Bash Automation • Python REST APIs • Cloud Infrastructure | Builder of Developer Productivity Toolkit`
- **Option 2 (Full-Stack & Cloud Focus):**  
  `Full-Stack Developer & Cloud Enthusiast | Building Resilient Systems, Telemetry Dashboards & Automation Pipelines (Linux • Python • Bash • JS)`
- **Option 3 (Student / Graduate Focus):**  
  `Computer Science Engineer | Passionate about Systems Engineering, CI/CD Automation & Linux Internals | Open to DevOps & Software Engineering Roles`
